Search Intelligence: Predicting Organic Content Decay & Automated Refresh Prioritization

Author: Taha Ahmed • FlyRank ML Research Track • Lane: Refresh Opportunity Scoring
Abstract

Organic search performance decays continuously as search intent evolves and competing domains publish fresher topical coverage. This study introduces an automated opportunity scoring architecture designed to forecast significant traffic collapse on high-impression search assets 14 days in advance. Utilizing DuckDB to perform temporal aggregations over warehouse search telemetry, we engineered multi-scale velocity trends, position volatility signals, and rolling click-through decay metrics without forward leakage. A calibrated tree-based ensemble achieved an empirical ROC-AUC of 0.814, outperforming naive heuristic baselines while drastically reducing probabilistic calibration error. Model output distributions are mapped directly to an automated editorial playbook with granular diagnostic reason codes to guide preemptive content refreshes.

Problem Statement & Decision Support

Modern digital publishing teams face a severe resource allocation dilemma: content refreshes are reactive, executed only after significant organic visibility has already collapsed. This capstone constructs a proactive decision-support engine that scores and prioritizes at-risk URLs, directing editorial interventions where ranking slippage is most reversible.

Primary Objective
14-Day Forecast
Detect pages undergoing ≥20% click contraction before full traffic drops.
Model Utility
Priority Queue
Rank-orders URLs by opportunity score paired with targeted remediation actions.

Data & Public-Safe Aggregation

The research utilized the official FlyRank Search Intelligence warehouse release via DuckDB over remote parquet partitions. To strictly preserve privacy and security standards:

  • Zero PII or URL Strings: All domain names, customer identities, and raw query keywords were omitted and replaced with synthetic hash surrogates.
  • Volume Filtering: Low-volume pages with fewer than 50 total impressions were pruned to eliminate high-variance noise.
  • Feature Representation: Extracted metrics include 30-day impression momentum, 14-day position volatility, and rolling CTR relative decay rates.

Methodology & Validation Design

To prevent temporal data leakage, we implemented a strict forward-chaining time-aware split rather than random cross-validation. The training partition encompasses historical observations, while the validation evaluation window strictly scores unseen future temporal windows.

Target Definition: y = 1 if (Clicks_next_14d / Clicks_past_14d) < 0.80 else 0 Validation Strategy: Time-Aware Temporal Cutoff (Strict Separation, Zero Leakage)

Ranked Action Playbook

Predicted probabilities are transformed into normalized Opportunity Scores (0–100) and paired with automated diagnostic reason codes:

ACT-01

CTR Decay Under High Visibility (Metadata & Intent Mismatch)

High impression volume with sharp CTR degradation. Action: Overhaul Title Tags, refine SERP meta description, and verify search intent alignment.

ACT-02

Ranking Slippage (Topical Freshness & Content Depth)

Average SERP position dropping outside top 10 rankings. Action: Update outdated statistics, add missing subheadings, and inject authoritative internal links.

ACT-03

Topical Stagnation (General Decay & Pruning)

Gradual cross-metric contraction over 3 consecutive rolling windows. Action: Execute comprehensive editorial revamp or consolidate with overlapping topical hubs.

Limitations & Honest Framing

In accordance with empirical machine learning ethics, this scoring framework serves as an observed directional decision-support tool. The system identifies historical statistical patterns associated with traffic loss; it does not claim causal discovery of Google's proprietary search algorithms nor does it guarantee automated ranking recovery.
